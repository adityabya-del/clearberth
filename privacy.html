Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ClearBerth ("the app," "we," "us") is a train seat availability checker for Indian Railways. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following directly on your phone, using local storage. This information is never transmitted to us or any server we control:

  • Your recent searches (from, to, date, quota)
  • Your favorite routes
  • Your display preferences (dark mode, language, accent color)

You can clear this information at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your phone's settings.

Information sent to third parties

To show you train availability, PNR status, and live train status, the app sends the details you enter (such as station codes, travel dates, train numbers, or PNR numbers) to a third-party data provider that supplies this information. We do not control how that provider handles data beyond fulfilling your request; we recommend reviewing their own privacy practices if you have questions.

Sharing features

The app includes buttons to share trip details via WhatsApp, SMS, or your phone's general share options. These open your phone's own messaging apps with a pre-filled message; you choose whether and to whom to send it. ClearBerth does not see or store who you share with.

Booking

ClearBerth does not process bookings or payments. The "Book on IRCTC" button opens IRCTC's official website in your browser, where IRCTC's own privacy policy and terms apply.

What we do not collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or log in.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, or email address.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, camera, or photos.
  • We do not currently use analytics tracking.

Changes to this policy

If ClearBerth's data practices change (for example, if accounts or analytics are added in the future), this page will be updated accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact details listed on our GitHub repository.
