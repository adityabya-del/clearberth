# ClearBerth

ClearBerth is a free Indian Railways app for checking train seat availability, PNR status and live train running status, with optional journey alerts. Available in English and Hindi.

**Website:** [clearberth.app](https://clearberth.app)

## Get the app

- [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/clearberth/id6802574946)
- [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aditya.clearberth)

## What it does

- **Seat availability:** search by route, date, class and quota to see berth availability across trains.
- **Nearby stations:** when a route has no trains, suggests trains from nearby stations on the same route.
- **PNR status:** check your PNR and see how your booking is moving.
- **Live train status:** see where a train is running and how late it is.
- **Journey alerts (optional, paid):** PNR status alerts and live journey alerts as push notifications.
- **Daily tip:** one railway tip a day around noon, silent, drawn from IRCTC and Indian Railways information.
- **Book on IRCTC:** jump straight to IRCTC's official site to complete a booking.

ClearBerth does not sell tickets. Bookings are made on IRCTC.
