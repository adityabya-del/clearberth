Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ClearBerth ("the app," "we," "us") shows PNR status, live train tracking, and journey alerts for Indian Railways. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following directly on your phone, using local storage:

  • Your recent searches and display preferences (dark mode, language, accent color)
  • Your favorite routes

You can clear this at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your phone's settings.

Information sent to our servers

When you check a PNR or train number, that number is sent to our servers, which query a third-party data provider to return the result to you. We store this search history, tied to your device's push notification token, so we can show you results and, if you subscribe, send you alerts about that specific journey.

If you subscribe to journey alerts, we store the PNR or train number, your chosen journey date, and your push notification token for as long as needed to deliver those alerts, after which the subscription is automatically deactivated.

We also log IP addresses on our servers, used only to prevent abuse of our systems, and record which in-app buttons you interact with (such as tapping a food-delivery link), to understand general app usage.

The information we store on our servers, described above, is not sold and is not used by us for advertising or tracking. The ads shown in the app are provided by Google and are described under "Advertising" below.

Daily tips

If you allow notifications, we store your push notification token, your chosen language, your device type (iPhone or Android) and your app version on our servers, so we can send you one railway tip a day, around noon. Tips make no sound. You can stop them at any time by turning off ClearBerth notifications in your phone's settings; on Android you can also turn off only the "ClearBerth Tips" channel. If you uninstall the app, the token is removed automatically once it stops working. To have it deleted sooner, email us at clearberth.support@gmail.com.

In-app purchases

ClearBerth offers optional paid features (PNR Status Alerts, Live Journey Alerts). Payments are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play — we never see or store your payment details.

Advertising

ClearBerth is free to use and shows ads (banner, medium-rectangle, interstitial and rewarded ads) provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and device and app information, under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive this data and do not pass the information we store on our servers to Google for advertising.

On iPhone, the app asks for your permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites for advertising; if you decline, ads are not personalized using that tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your Google settings (Settings > Google > Ads). You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party services

The Food tab links to Zomato's and Swiggy's own official pages, and the "Book on IRCTC" button opens IRCTC's official website. These are third-party services with their own privacy policies; ClearBerth does not collect data from your activity on those pages. The app also opens your phone's own SMS/messaging apps with a pre-filled message for certain features; you choose whether and to whom to send it, and ClearBerth does not see or store this.

What we do not collect

  • ClearBerth has no account or sign-in system.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, or email address.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, camera, or photos.

Data deletion

To request that your data be deleted, email us at clearberth.support@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If ClearBerth's data practices change, this page will be updated accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to clearberth.support@gmail.com.
